# Social Distribution

## Why This Matters

Social media provides:
- Quick feedback loop on what resonates
- Direct access to target audience (fintwit, reddit investors)
- Viral potential for interesting findings
- Backlinks that help SEO

## Strategy 1: Twitter/X Presence

### Account Setup
- Handle: @whatsfiled or @whatsfiledapp
- Bio: "Real-time insider trading tracker. Free Form 4 data."
- Link to homepage

### Content Strategy

**Automated Posts (High Volume)**
- Notable insider buys (P code, > $100k)
- Cluster buying (multiple insiders at same company)
- CEO/CFO transactions (higher signal)

Example tweet format:
```
📈 $AAPL Insider Buy

Tim Cook purchased 50,000 shares ($8.7M)
First open-market buy by CEO since 2021

Details: whatsfiled.com/filing/xxxxx
```

**Manual Posts (Engagement)**
- Weekly roundups with commentary
- Interesting patterns you notice
- Reply to fintwit discussions about stocks with relevant insider data

### Tools
- Buffer or Typefully for scheduling
- Build simple automation: DB trigger → tweet on notable transactions
- Or manual curation to start (higher quality, more work)

### Accounts to Study
- @unusual_whales - high engagement, good format
- @insikitrading - simple, consistent
- @secfilings - straightforward filing alerts

## Strategy 2: Reddit

### Target Subreddits
- r/stocks (3.5M members) - serious investors
- r/investing (2.2M) - long-term focused
- r/wallstreetbets (15M) - high volume, meme-friendly
- r/SecurityAnalysis (200k) - professional, high quality

### Content Approach

**DO:**
- Share genuinely interesting findings
- Provide context and analysis, not just links
- Engage in comments
- Be transparent that it's your product

**DON'T:**
- Spam links
- Post without adding value
- Create fake accounts to shill

### Post Examples

Good post title: "I built a free tool to track insider trading - here's what insiders are buying this month"

Include:
- Brief explanation of what you built
- Interesting data/findings
- Link to the tool
- Ask for feedback

## Strategy 3: Finance Communities

### Hacker News
- Post when you have something technically interesting
- "Show HN: I built a free insider trading tracker"
- Best for: developer angle, technical implementation details

### Discord Servers
- Stock trading discords
- Indie hacker communities
- Offer value first, mention product naturally

### Substack / Newsletter
- "WhatsFiled Weekly" - curated insider activity
- Cross-promote with other finance substacks
- Builds email list (owned audience)

## Strategy 4: Influencer Seeding

### Target
- Finance YouTubers
- Fintwit accounts with 10k-100k followers
- Newsletter writers

### Approach
- Reach out offering free access (if you add paid features later)
- Offer to provide data/charts for their content
- Don't ask for promotion directly, just be useful

## Measuring Success

- Twitter: Followers, engagement rate, link clicks
- Reddit: Upvotes, traffic from reddit (check analytics)
- Overall: Referral traffic in analytics

Target: 1,000 Twitter followers in 3 months
